## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory, comforting world of artisanal baking and specialty coffee roasting. It merges **Modern Rustic** craft with quiet, organic minimalism. The digital presence is tactile and inviting, eschewing cold corporate starkness in favor of sunlit, parchment-inspired textures, rich earth tones, and warm human precision.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Artisanal Integrity & Slow Living:** Celebrates process, fermentation, origin, and mindful consumption.
- **Warm & Hospitable:** Elicits the feeling of stepping into an aromatic neighborhood bakery at dawn, surrounded by freshly pulled espresso and steaming sourdough.
- **Organic Modernism:** Structured layouts paired with soft curves, natural parchment backdrops, and grounded botanical accents.

### Visual Style Direction
A curated fusion of **Modern Tactile Minimal** and **Editorial Warmth**:
- Ample, breathable negative space layered over warm neutral canvas tones (`#FBF8F2`) rather than sterile blue-whites.
- Deep espresso ink contrast replacing pure digital blacks.
- Generous photography moments focusing on natural lighting, flour dusting, golden crust caramelization, and ceramic pottery.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the roasting of single-origin beans, cinnamon fermentation, terracotta pottery, and botanical accents found in sunlit herb gardens.

- **Primary (`#2C1810` - Roasted Espresso):** A profound, rich dark brown with warm undertones. Used for primary typography, authoritative actions, deep surface treatments, and primary navigational framing. Replaces pure black entirely.
- **Secondary (`#C47A53` - Terracotta Caramel):** A warm, spiced clay tone. Used for active badges, accent buttons, pull quotes, highlight ribbons, and price emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#5C6B50` - Botanical Sage):** An earthy, muted olive-sage. Used to communicate freshness, organic harvest certifications, dietary badges (vegan, gluten-free), and seasonal offerings.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF8F2` - Soft Parchment) & Secondary Neutral (`#F4ECE1` - Oatmeal Crema):** Forms the foundational background and card tiering. Soft on the eyes, offering an organic paper-like ground.
- **Muted Earth (`#8D5B4C` - Roasted Cinnamon):** Reserved for secondary body text, subtle borders, and inactive states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the literary, artisanal heritage of high-contrast transitional serifs with the clarity and accessibility of an organic neo-grotesque sans-serif.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the story of craftsmanship, origin, and seasonal selections. Titles should use italic flourishes sparingly on key culinary descriptors (e.g., *single-origin*, *sourdough*).
- **Body & Product Content (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Features rounded terminals and open apertures, maintaining warmth and legibility across tasting notes, allergen callouts, brewing instructions, and transactional checkout flows.
- **Labels, Tags & Microcopy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Set with a slightly wider letter-spacing in medium to semibold weights, optimizing quick glanceability for menu categories and coffee origin specs (altitude, process, varietal).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a thoughtful, editorial rhythm with generous negative space, mirroring the tranquil ambiance of a slow-coffee counter.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid, max content container width of 1280px, with `3rem` (48px) exterior margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Elements anchor cleanly in editorial pairs (e.g., 7-column story, 5-column sensory notes).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.25rem` (20px) gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins, and `1rem` (16px) gutters. Complex product listings collapse from horizontal cards into single-column vertical cards.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm relies on loose spacing clusters: tight relationships within product cards (`space-xs` to `space-sm`), comfortable breathing room inside card bodies (`space-md` to `space-lg`), and expansive separation between menu sections or editorial stories (`space-xl` and above).

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the organic, tactile identity, depth is rendered through **tonal layering and warm, diffuse ambient illumination** rather than artificial, stark drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#FBF8F2` (Soft Parchment).
  - **Level 1 (Cards, Menu Items, Elevated Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F4ECE1` with a fine, low-contrast ghost outline: `1px solid rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.07)`.
  - **Level 2 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Action Baskets):** Elevated with an ambient warm shadow tinted with roasted espresso tones: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.04)`.
- **Tactile Touchpoints:** Avoid high-gloss or harsh digital borders. All component edges feel pressed into card stock or handmade ceramics.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances rustic softness with modern refinement using level `2` (Rounded) geometry:
- **Default Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius, conveying organic friendliness without appearing childish or bubbly.
- **Container & Card Elements (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) radius, softly framing bakery photography and daily roast menus.
- **Hero Banners & Modal Dialogs (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) radius, evoking smooth ceramic dishware and sculpted dough contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2C1810` background with `#FBF8F2` typography. Soft hover state shifts subtly to a rich roasted tone `#3E2723` with a micro-scale translation (elevation of 1px).
- **Secondary / Accent:** `#C47A53` terracotta fill with `#FFFFFF` text, used for primary order flows, cart additions, or subscription signups.
- **Outline / Editorial:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #2C1810`, text in `#2C1810`. Hover creates an oatmeal fill `#F4ECE1`.
- **Text Action:** Bold label typography in `#C47A53` with an understated organic underline offset by 4px.

### Chips & Badges
- **Origin & Flavor Badges:** Pill-style or softly rounded badges with neutral-crema background (`#F4ECE1`), text in `#8D5B4C` (`label-sm`).
- **Dietary & Botanical Tags:** Subtle sage background (`rgba(92, 107, 80, 0.12)`) paired with `#5C6B50` text for organic, sourdough, vegan, and fair-trade badges.

### Cards
- **Bakery & Coffee Showcase Cards:** Rendered on white or crema (`#F4ECE1`) surfaces with `rounded-lg` corners and delicate `1px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.06)` borders. Imagery sits flush at the top with a subtle 4:3 or 1:1 organic aspect ratio.
- **Sensory Tasting Note Cards:** Divided into clear tasting profiles (Acidity, Body, Notes) with clean vertical dividers in `#8D5B4C` at 15% opacity.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** `#FBF8F2` or pure `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.18)` border. On focus, smoothly transitions to `1.5px solid #C47A53` with a warm glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(196, 122, 83, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom rounded boxes and circles. Checked state fills with `#2C1810` or `#C47A53` and reveals an off-white organic check mark.

### Menu & Item Lists
- **Editorial Menu Rows:** Product title in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`), price formatted in `label-lg` aligned right, connected by an understated dotted or hairline leader in `rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.12)`, followed by description in `body-sm` (`#8D5B4C`).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Flavors Wheel / Radar Tagging:** Visual sensory capsules displaying notes such as "Honeysuckle", "Orange Blossom", "Dark Chocolate", or "Cardamom".
- **Fermentation & Brew Guides:** Step-by-step accordion modules featuring soft progress lines colored in botanical sage (`#5C6B50`).
